# Chaos to Clarity Skills

Claude skills for founders and operators, from Eric Weiss at [Chaos to Clarity](https://chaostoclarity.io). Each one turns a recurring piece of work into a single message to Claude.

Want help putting this to work in your company? [Work with Eric.](#work-with-eric)

### Cortex Suite

Skills that run on your [Cortex](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex), the context layer that holds your people, companies, initiatives, decisions, and strategy. Each one reads it, does the work, and writes back what it learned, so every skill gets smarter as the others run. Install Cortex first.

| Plugin | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [**Cortex**](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex) | The context layer your AI reads first. A Map, catalogs, and records for your people, initiatives, decisions, and strategy, in Notion, Obsidian, or Google Drive. Lives in its own repo, listed here so one marketplace installs everything. |
| [**Cortex Gnome**](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex/tree/main/plugins/cortex-gnome) | The overnight worker. Every night it turns the day's context and tagged tasks into finished work: decks, docs, landing pages, research, code with pull requests, drafts. It logs everything, sends a brief, and learns from your feedback. Teams can run several. Lives in the Cortex repo; also works without a Cortex. **Costs tokens every night whether or not it makes anything useful, and is only as good as your feedback.** Starts reined in and earns autonomy as you keep its work. Best on high-limit plans such as Claude Max. |
| [**Founder OS**](plugins/founder-os) | Your weekly operating cycle. Plan Monday, brief each morning, reflect Friday. |
| [**Investor Update**](plugins/investor-update) | Your monthly investor update, drafted from your Cortex. Metrics with deltas, highlights, required lowlights, what you promised last time, and specific asks. Always a draft. |
| [**Customer Signal**](plugins/customer-signal) | Every customer and prospect call becomes evidence. Pains, requests, objections, churn risks, and praise, counted across accounts, with a weekly brief and close-the-loop notes when you ship. |
| [**One-on-Ones**](plugins/one-on-ones) | Prep and debrief every 1:1 with your direct reports. Commitments both ways, recurring themes, a health flag you set, and a monthly team review. Private by design. |
| [**Meeting Recap**](plugins/recap) | Every meeting becomes done work: a branded recap email, your to-dos with time booked, follow-ups drafted, agreed meetings booked, and context saved. Runs twice a day. Also works without a Cortex. |
| [**Content Engine**](plugins/content-engine) | Last week's meetings become this week's content. One original framework, a newsletter issue, and a LinkedIn post in your voice, with every story kept confidential. Runs every Monday. Also works without a Cortex. |
| [**Handwritten Notes**](plugins/handwritten-notes) | Photograph a page of notes. Checkbox items become tasks with time booked on your calendar. Everything else is filed as notes, linked to the right people and projects in your Cortex. Also works without a Cortex. |

Start with Cortex, then add Founder OS and whichever of the rest fit how you work. Founder OS, Investor Update, Customer Signal, and One-on-Ones need a Cortex. Meeting Recap, Content Engine, Handwritten Notes, and Cortex Gnome run on their own and get smarter with one. Add Cortex Gnome last, once your context and tools are in place and you have time to review its first two weeks of work.

### Chaos to Clarity Coach

Thirty leadership exercises from ten years of coaching venture-backed founders. Describe the problem in one sentence; Claude picks the tool, tells you why, and coaches you through it one question at a time. No Cortex needed.

Install the AI Coach for all 30 behind one front door, or install only the themes you need. Each theme points you to the right tool in another theme when your problem belongs there.

| Plugin | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [**Chaos to Clarity AI Coach**](plugins/c2c-coach) | All 30 tools. Not sure where your problem sits? Start here. |
| [**Focus and Decisions**](plugins/c2c-coach-focus) | Nine tools for when you're the constraint: find the one priority, break a bottleneck, reset when anxious, and make the call you keep circling. |
| [**Leading People**](plugins/c2c-coach-people) | Six tools for your team: name what you tolerate, have the hard conversation, delegate so work comes back right, settle who decides, and hire executives well. |
| [**Co-Founder and Board**](plugins/c2c-coach-cofounder) | Three tools for the power relationships: CEO and CTO decision rights, a co-founder fight that keeps looping, and a tense board or overreaching investors. |
| [**Org and Scaling**](plugins/c2c-coach-scale) | Six tools for a company outgrowing heroics: an operating rhythm, clean team ownership, a scorecard someone owns, and what breaks as you grow. |
| [**Growth Plays**](plugins/c2c-coach-growth) | Four tools for growth that feels forced: customer discovery, pull instead of paid, a new market, and a launch people notice. |
| [**Facilitator Kit**](plugins/c2c-coach-facilitator) | Two tools for running the room: founder hot seats for peer groups, advisors, or exec teams, and team retros that end in action. |

## Updating

Plugins added from the marketplace in the Claude app pick up new versions on their own. In Claude Code, turn on auto-update for the marketplace under `/plugin`, then Marketplaces, or run `claude plugin update`. For single skills, download links always serve the newest version: delete the old skill in Claude's Skills settings and upload the new ZIP. Your settings carry over, because they live in your own tools, not in the skill file.

## For maintainers

**Layout.** `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json` lists the plugins. Each plugin lives in `plugins/<plugin>/` with `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` and one folder per skill in `skills/`. Shared reference files are mastered in `shared/` and copied into the skills that use them by `scripts/sync_shared.py`.

```
.claude-plugin/marketplace.json   the plugin list
plugins/<plugin>/                 one folder per plugin, each with skills/<skill>/SKILL.md
shared/                           files mastered once and copied into skills
shared/coach/                     every Chaos to Clarity Coach tool
scripts/                          sync_shared.py and build_zips.py
```

**Adding a skill**

1. Add `plugins/<plugin>/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`. The folder name must match the skill's `name` (lowercase, hyphens), and names must be unique across plugins.
2. Keep the `description` under 200 characters, and keep personal details (IDs, emails, workspace links) out of the file.
3. For a new plugin, add its `plugin.json` and an entry in `marketplace.json`. Don't set `version`: leaving it out lets installs track each commit.
4. Add a row to the download table above.
5. Commit and push to `main`. The release workflow checks everything, builds the ZIPs, and publishes a release. Links update automatically.

**Shared files.** Cortex Suite skills carry shared references: `cortex-spec.md`, fetched from the [Cortex repo](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex/blob/main/spec/cortex-spec.md), plus `founder-os.md`, `recap-config.md`, and `content-engine-config.md`, mastered in `shared/`. Run `python scripts/sync_shared.py` to fetch and copy them. The release workflow also runs it daily and commits any change, so these plugins pick up Cortex spec updates on their own. A new Cortex Suite plugin gets its copies by adding it to `TARGETS` in `scripts/sync_shared.py`.

**Coach tools.** Every Chaos to Clarity Coach tool is mastered once in `shared/coach/`: the exercise in `tools/<slug>.md`, its problems and founder phrases in `index/<slug>.md`, and the shared coaching method in `coach-method.md`. `shared/coach/coach.json` names each tool and assigns it to a theme. The sync copies every tool into the AI Coach and into its theme plugin, builds each skill's `references/tool-index.md`, and fills the generated table between the `coach-index` markers in each `SKILL.md`. Never edit a coach skill's `references/` folder or that table by hand; the release check fails if they drift.

- **Edit a tool:** change `shared/coach/tools/<slug>.md` or `shared/coach/index/<slug>.md`, then run the sync.
- **Move a tool to another theme:** move its slug in `coach.json`, update both themes' "Help me pick" lists in their `SKILL.md`, then run the sync. Stale copies are removed.
- **Add a tool:** add both files and a `coach.json` entry, add it to the theme's "Help me pick" list and the AI Coach's triage, then run the sync.
- **Refer to tools by name, never by number.** In an index fragment, write `[[slug]]` and the sync renders the name, plus its plugin when the tool lives in another theme.

**Cortex** and **Cortex Gnome** are listed in `marketplace.json` with `git-subdir` sources pointing at `Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex`. Their code lives only there.

**Test locally**

```
python scripts/sync_shared.py
python scripts/build_zips.py
claude plugin validate .
```
