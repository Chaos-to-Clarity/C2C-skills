# Chaos to Clarity Coach: Focus and Decisions

For the founder who is the constraint: too many priorities, a stuck company, a call you keep putting off, or a week where anxiety is making the decisions.

Tell Claude what's going on in one sentence. It picks the right tool, tells you why, and coaches you through it one question at a time. It pushes back when you're vague, names what you're avoiding, and ends with a commitment, not a pep talk.

## The tools

| Tool | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Single Domino Compression Tool | You're overwhelmed and can't name the one thing that matters most |
| 30-Minute Bottleneck Breakthrough | Everything feels stuck and you can't name the one constraint |
| Identity Ceiling Tool | The same problem keeps coming back and you suspect it's you |
| Founder Emotional Reset Framework | Anxiety, reactivity, or burnout is driving your decisions |
| Six-Month Founder Clarity Exercise | You're starting a new half or chapter and your goals are vague |
| Time Machine Vision Exercise | You can't describe where the company is in two years, or the team has no picture of winning |
| Domino Strategy Exercise | You have too many initiatives with no sequence |
| High-Stakes Decision Protocol | You keep circling a big call: fire, raise, pivot, or sell |
| VUCA Leadership Navigator | The ground keeps shifting and you can't frame what kind of problem you're facing |

## Get started

Install the plugin (see the [main page](../../README.md#install-the-whole-set-recommended)), then describe your problem, say **"help me pick,"** or name a tool: **"Run the Single Domino Compression Tool."**

This is one of six themed slices of the [Chaos to Clarity AI Coach](../c2c-coach), which carries all 30 tools. Install the AI Coach instead if you want everything behind one front door. When a problem fits a tool in another slice, this one tells you which.
