# One-on-Ones

Prep and debrief every 1:1 with your direct reports, from your [Cortex](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex).

Most founders walk into 1:1s cold. They forget what they promised last time, miss that the same blocker has come up four weeks running, and let the meeting slide when the week gets busy. One-on-Ones keeps a private record for each direct report. It holds what they owe you, what you owe them, the themes they keep raising, their growth focus, and a health flag you set. Each morning you get a prep for that day's 1:1s.

It's built to push. What you owe comes first. Patterns that keep repeating get named, along with the [Chaos to Clarity AI Coach](../c2c-coach) tool that fits, such as the Toleration Audit or the Hard Conversation Protocol.

## How it works

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Once | Finds your 1:1s on your calendar, confirms your direct reports, asks for each one's growth focus, and schedules the routines |
| Each work day, on a schedule | Debriefs yesterday's 1:1s from your meeting notes, then emails you the prep for today's |
| After a 1:1 | Say **"debrief my 1:1 with Sam"** and talk it through, or let the daily run read the notes |
| Monthly | A team review: everyone's health, missed 1:1s, overdue commitments both ways, themes the whole team is raising, and one move |

## Private by design

Nothing from a 1:1 leaves your Team records and your own inbox. It never appears in recaps, investor updates, content, or anything shared. Health, family, compensation, and anything said in confidence are never stored. Health is the only judgment in a record, and you set it. If you use Recap, setup offers to hand your 1:1s over to One-on-Ones so a report never gets a recap email.

Anyone with access to your Cortex can read the Team records, so keep it private.

## Get started

1. Install the Cortex and One-on-Ones plugins (see the [main page](../../README.md)). One-on-Ones needs a Cortex with your calendar connected.
2. Say: **"Set up my Cortex"** if you haven't, then **"Prep my 1:1s."** The first run sets it up.

## What it needs

- A Cortex, with your calendar connected. Meeting notes make debriefs automatic; without them, you talk it through after.
- For the morning prep and the monthly review: a Claude plan with scheduled tasks

Works well with **Founder OS** (your 1:1 to-dos land in your tasks), **Recap**, and the **Chaos to Clarity AI Coach**.
