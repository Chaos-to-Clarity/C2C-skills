# Habit Library

Starter habits for drafting during intake and habit reviews. Never hand the whole list over. Pick the two or three that fit what the user said, rewrite them in their words, and let them edit. The user's own habits always win over these.

Each entry: the habit, a sample Target, and the short checkbox name for Daily Check-ins.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Build | Eat for energy | Meals built around protein and vegetables; fruit for snacks | Ate Well |
| Build | Water | A full bottle by lunch and another by dinner | Water |
| Drop | Drinking past the line | More than the agreed nights or drinks per week | Alcohol Held |
| Drop | Mindless snacking | Eating out of the bag at the desk or in front of a screen | (tracked by Ate Well) |

Keep food habits about energy and quality. Never set calorie, weight, or restriction targets. If food becomes a source of distress, drop food tracking and suggest professional support.
