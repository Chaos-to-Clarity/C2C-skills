# Weekly Session

About 30 minutes, on the weekly session day, in place of that day's check-in. It recaps the week from the data, rescans every area with two numbers, tunes the habits, goes deep on one topic, and sets one commitment for the week.

**Like the intake, this session fails when it ends early.** A strong deep topic is not the end of the session. Check the map before any wrap-up language.

## Prepare (silently)

1. Read the home (Settings, Who I'm becoming, Active commitments, Coaching rules, Parking lot, Weekly session agenda, the last Session notes entry), the Coaching Frameworks page, and all Active habits.
2. Query Daily Check-ins since the last weekly session (or the last 7 days) and the last four Weekly Sessions rows.
3. Compute, from the data: each area's score at the start and end of the week, the average, habit hit rates, the commitment's record, missed check-ins, and which habit misses lined up with which score drops (and which hits lined up with rises).
4. Create today's Weekly Sessions row (Session "YYYY-MM-DD Weekly", Type Weekly, Status Missed) and today's Daily Check-ins row.

## The map

Show it in the first message after the opener. In every message after, mark progress: "Step 4 of 10: scan (Partner, 4 of 6)."

| # | Step | Done when | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tonight and yesterday | Tonight's plan and yesterday's habits are logged | 2 min |
| 2 | Week recap | They've seen the data and given their read | 4 min |
| 3 | Commitment check | Each active commitment is marked held or broken, and any break is understood | 3 min |
| 4 | Scan | Every area has a 7-day and a 90-day score | 4 min |
| 5 | Habit review | The weakest area's habits are kept, changed, added, or retired | 4 min |
| 6 | Pick the deep topic | They've chosen from the agenda and parking lot | 1 min |
| 7 | Deep topic | One topic worked through GROW, with an insight in their words | 8 to 12 min |
| 8 | Commitment for the week | One commitment with trigger, replacement, a Will score of 8 or more, and how the check-in watches it | 2 min |
| 9 | Calibrate | Daily check-in changes agreed (or none) | 1 min |
| 10 | Close | They've said what was most useful and what they learned; everything is saved | 1 min |

Rules from `system.md` section 8 apply: one question per message, detours with a return to the map, a resume point if they stop, never close early.

## Step 1. Tonight and yesterday

The opener reaches a phone: one line ("Weekly session today, about 30 minutes.") and the tonight question from the daily check-in. Then yesterday's habit checklist. Log both.

## Step 2. Week recap

Give the readout in one message, from the data:

- Each area: start, end, and direction ("Sleep 4 to 7, up").
- Habit hit rates, keystone first ("Screens off 5 of 7, Exercise 3 of 7").
- The connections: which habits lined up with which moves ("Sleep was 7 or better every morning after screens went off by 9, and 4 or worse the two nights they didn't").
- Missed check-ins, if any.

Then ask: **"What's your read on the week?"** Save Week Recap and Habit Hit Rates.

## Step 3. Commitment check

For each active commitment: held or broken? If broken, find the exact moment, what they told themselves, and whether it was a permission slip. Use *Above the Chaos* fact versus story, or Atomic Habits' never miss twice. If it held all week, say so plainly and ask what made it hold.

## Step 4. Scan

One area per message: **"[Area]: last 7 days, and the 90-day trend."** One-word acknowledgment between areas, nothing more. After the last area, one line on the biggest gap between the two numbers. Log every score.

## Step 5. Habit review

Take the weakest area (lowest score, or biggest drop). Show its habits and their hit rates. Diagnose any failing habit against the four laws of Atomic Habits (is the cue missing, is it too hard, is the bad option too easy?). Ask: **"Keep, change, add, or retire?"** Update the Habits collection, and add or retire a Daily Check-ins column if needed.

## Step 6. Pick the deep topic

Start with their agenda: **"What's on your mind?"** Then show the top of the Weekly session agenda and anything new in the Parking lot. If what's on their mind isn't on the list, it usually wins. Otherwise recommend one, based on the data and what's been parked longest. Let them choose.

## Step 7. Deep topic

Work one topic for 8 to 12 minutes on GROW, with the framework the situation router points to for content. One question at a time:

- **Goal:** "What do you want from this, today and beyond?"
- **Reality:** what's actually happening, the facts versus the story, what they've already tried, what's under it, where behavior and stated values split. Stay here until it's clear. If they're circling, ask "What's the real challenge here for you?"
- **Options:** "What could you do?" then "And what else?" Offer a framework idea only after their own options are out.
- **Will:** carries into step 8.

Listen at level 3 (tone, what isn't said, gaps between their numbers and their words) and name what you notice once. On painful topics, let them set the pace and stop when they ask.

A full exercise is welcome here: *Above the Chaos* Exercise 5 (Emotional Response Analysis) for anger or reactivity, Exercise 1 (Control and Influence Mapping) for overwhelm or avoidance, Exercise 4 (Virtue Assessment) for a values check. If an exercise runs long, it's fine to finish it next week; save where it stopped.

Close the topic by asking for the insight in their words. Save Deep Topic and Insight.

## Step 8. Commitment for the week

One specific action from the deep topic or the habit review, with the trigger moment, the replacement or first step, what will get in the way, and how the daily check-in should watch it. Check Will: **"How likely are you to do this, 1 to 10?"** Under 8, shrink it. Ask what they're saying no to by saying yes to it. Add it to Commitments (Status Active) and Active commitments on the home. If an older commitment is now a steady habit, offer to move it to the Habits collection.

## Step 9. Calibrate

Ask: **"Anything to keep, drop, or add in the daily check-ins?"** Apply changes to the home's protocol, the habit columns, or the scheduled task.

## Step 10. Close

Check the map. If any step from 1 to 9 isn't done or explicitly skipped, go back to it. Then ask: **"What was most useful for you today?"** and **"What did you learn about yourself this week?"**

Then save:
- The Weekly Sessions row: all scores, Week Recap, Habit Hit Rates, Deep Topic, Insight, Commitment, Check-in Changes, Status.
- The home: update the Weekly session agenda (remove what was covered, add what came up), Active commitments, the Parking lot, and add a short dated Session notes entry.

## Monthly zoom-out

On the first weekly session of each month, replace step 5 with a zoom-out (10 minutes):

- Read the identity statements aloud. Ask which one they lived best this month and which one they lived least.
- Show the month's trend per area from the Weekly Sessions rows.
- Offer one area they don't track yet (Money, Friends and Community, Purpose) or a one-year vision for one area. Add it only if they want it.

Set the row's Type to Monthly.
