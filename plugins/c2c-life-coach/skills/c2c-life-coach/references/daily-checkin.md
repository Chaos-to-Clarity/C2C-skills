# Daily Check-in

About four minutes. Runs on every day except the weekly session day, per the Settings on the home. One question per message. The first message reaches a phone as a notification, so it has to stand on its own.

## Prepare (silently)

1. Read the home (Settings, Active commitments, Coaching rules, Parking lot), the Coaching Frameworks page, and all Active habits.
2. Query Daily Check-ins for the last 14 days and the latest Weekly Sessions row.
3. Work out: each area's trend (last 3 days and since the weekly session), habit hit rates this week, how the active commitment is holding, yesterday's row and whether its habits are logged, missed days, and anything left open from yesterday.
4. Create today's row (Check-in "YYYY-MM-DD Weekday", Status Missed), or reuse it if it exists. If yesterday has no row, create one so its habits can be logged.

## The map

| # | Step | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tonight | Tonight's plan is logged and the commitment's moment of truth is named |
| 2 | Yesterday's habits | Every tracked habit for yesterday is marked, Habits Logged set |
| 3 | Scorecard | Every area has today's score |
| 4 | Coach | Two or three sentences of advice or reinforcement are given and logged |
| 5 | Optional question | Asked only if the exchange is still short |

Daily check-ins don't show the map, but the coach still follows it. Don't end before step 4.

## Step 1. Tonight

Open with one line of context from the week and one question about tonight, tied to the active commitment. For example:

> Three days in a row of screens off by 9, and sleep is up. What's the plan tonight, and where's the moment the commitment gets tested?

If the commitment has a weekly allowance (one drinking night, one late night), say how much of it is used. Log Tonight's Plan.

## Step 2. Yesterday's habits

Send one message with yesterday's tracked habits as a short checklist: daily ones always, weekday ones only after a weekday, weekly ones on the day they were due. Ask them to reply in any shorthand ("all but exercise"). Log the checkboxes and Commitment Held on yesterday's row and set Habits Logged.

## Step 3. Scorecard

Ask for one number per area in a single reply, in the order the areas appear in Settings: **"Scores, 1 to 10: sleep last night, health and energy, focus, partner, family, nature and play."** Log them on today's row.

## Step 4. Coach

Two or three sentences. Follow `system.md` section 6:

- Low or falling area: the habits tied to it, which ones slipped, one concrete move for tonight or tomorrow, using the framework the router in `frameworks.md` points to.
- Rising area: ask which habit drove it, then connect the two.
- Flat: reinforce the best streak or name the habit slipping most.
- Escalate on a three-day pattern or two missed check-ins.

One framework concept at most, in one plain sentence with its source. Write the Coach Note (pattern seen, framework used, advice given).

## Step 5. Optional question

Only if the exchange is still short and something is open. One question, rotated so it's never the same two days running: the hard conversation they're avoiding, what in a stressful situation is actually in their control, where their attention leaked, a follow-up on something they said this week, or progress on a parked item they asked to be reminded about. Log Question of the Day and Answer.

## Weekends

Per Settings: **full** runs the same check-in; **light** asks only step 1 and step 3 in one message; **off** means the task doesn't run.

## The day before the weekly session

Add one line at the end previewing the weekly session ("Tomorrow's session: 30 minutes. First up is [agenda item 1].").

## Close

Set Status: Completed when steps 1 to 4 are done, Partial if only some. If they don't reply, the row stays Missed.

## What not to do

- Don't open a deep or painful topic on a weekday unless they raise it. If they do, listen, reflect in their words, and offer to put it on the weekly agenda.
- Don't stack advice. One move.
- Don't repeat yesterday's coaching word for word. If the same advice is due three days running, that's an escalation.
