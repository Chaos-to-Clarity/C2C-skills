# Chaos to Clarity Life Coach

A life coach that reaches out to you every day.

Most coaching happens once a week and fades by Wednesday. This one checks in daily, takes four minutes, and keeps the record, so you can see in your own data which habits move which parts of your life.

## How it works

- **A scorecard.** You score six areas of life outside work, 1 to 10: sleep, health and energy, time and focus, your partner, your family, nature and play. Add money, friends, or purpose if you want them.
- **Habits behind every score.** Each area has habits that build it and habits that damage it. Scores are the outputs; habits are the inputs.
- **A daily check-in.** At a time you choose, your coach asks about tonight and your commitment, checks yesterday's habits, takes your six scores, and gives you one piece of coaching. When a score drops, it names the habits behind it and one move to get back on track. When a score rises, it shows you which habit did it.
- **A weekly session.** About 30 minutes. A recap of the week from your own data, a fresh scan, a habit review, one deep topic, and one commitment for the week. Once a month it zooms out.
- **A real point of view.** The coaching draws on proven books, starting with *Above the Chaos*: Seneca on anger, Atomic Habits, Gottman on marriage, Nonviolent Communication, Emily Nagoski, The Self-Driven Child, Untangled, The Power of Showing Up, Outlive, Why We Sleep, This Naked Mind, Deep Work, Digital Minimalism, Essentialism, and Profit First. The core ideas from each are built in and applied to your habits.

## Get started

1. Install the plugin (see the [main page](../../README.md)).
2. Say **"Set up my life coach."** It asks whether you use a [Cortex](https://github.com/chaostoclarity/Cortex) and installs there, or wherever you choose (Notion, Google Drive, or a folder on your computer). Then it builds the system and schedules your daily check-in and weekly session.
3. Do the **intake** right after, about 45 minutes: score each area, map the habits that build and damage it, write who you're becoming, and set your first commitment. You can pause and pick up where you left off.

After that, it comes to you.

## What it needs

- A place to keep your data: a Cortex, Notion, Google Drive, or a connected folder
- A Claude plan with scheduled tasks, with the task allowed to run on its own, and notifications on

## Private by design

Everything you share stays in your own tools. Choose a private location: this holds your relationships, health, and habits. If your Cortex is shared with a team, setup will ask before installing there.

This is coaching, not therapy or medical care. If something comes up that needs a professional, your coach will say so.
