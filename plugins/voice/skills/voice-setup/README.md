# Voice Setup

Give Claude a few pieces of your own writing. You get back an analysis of how you write, a test piece in your voice, and, once it sounds right, your own voice skill that drafts and rewrites anything so it sounds like you.

**[Download voice-setup.zip](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/C2C-skills/releases/latest/download/voice-setup.zip)**

## What it does

- **Reads your samples.** Paste text, attach files, or share links. Three to five pieces you wrote yourself works best.
- **Analyzes your voice.** Tone, rhythm, word choice, how you open and close, your signature moves, and what you never do. Every point is backed by a quote or a measured number from your writing.
- **Writes a test piece** in your voice, on a topic you pick.
- **Iterates until you say it sounds like you.** Tell it what's off, or rewrite the test piece yourself. It diffs your edit line by line and turns every change into a rule.
- **Saves your voice skill.** A personal skill named after you (for example `maya-voice`) that you can call in any chat.

## What your voice skill does

- **Writes and rewrites** in your voice: "Draft a LinkedIn post in my voice about hiring my first VP," or "Rewrite this in my voice."
- **Catches AI tells** before you see the draft: "It's not X, it's Y" lines, lockstep lists, mic-drop endings, filler phrases, and the rest.
- **Learns from your edits.** Edit one of its drafts, paste your version, and say "learn from this." It finds the patterns in your changes and proposes updates to itself, with real before and after examples. Each draft needs fewer edits than the last.

## What you need

- **Claude** with skills turned on.
- **Samples of your writing**, about 1,500 words in total. Use writing that's yours: not ghostwritten, not heavily edited by someone else, not drafted by AI.

## First run

1. Say "set up my voice."
2. Share your samples and answer four quick questions: what you'll use it for, who reads it, what the test piece should be about, and any hard rules (no emojis, no em-dashes).
3. Read the analysis and the test piece. Accept it, ask for tweaks, or rewrite it yourself.
4. Once you accept, save your voice skill from the card Claude shows you. If there's no card, Claude gives you a ZIP to upload under Customize, Skills.

## Tips for good results

- **Pick your best work**, and the kind of writing you want help with. If you mostly want LinkedIn posts, include LinkedIn posts.
- **Include something that sounds wrong to you.** An AI draft you hated or a corporate version of your message shows Claude what to avoid.
- **Rewrite the test piece yourself** if it's close but not right. Your edits teach it more than any description.
- **Keep teaching it.** The first version is good. The version after ten of your edits is much better.
