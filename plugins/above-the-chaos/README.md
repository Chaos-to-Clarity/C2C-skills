# Above the Chaos

Nine Stoic leadership exercises for founders, from the book ***Above the Chaos*** by Eric Weiss.

The Stoics built their philosophy for people under pressure. These exercises apply it to running a company: where your attention belongs, how you meet setbacks, how you handle your own reactions, and what kind of leader you're becoming. Claude coaches you through each one, a question at a time, and gives you a clean summary with coaching notes and one thing to carry forward.

**Get the full book, with every exercise and the stories behind them, at [abovethechaosbook.com](https://abovethechaosbook.com).**

## The exercises

| # | Exercise | Principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Control and Influence Mapping | Focus on what you can control |
| 2 | Obstacle Transformation | Embrace obstacles as opportunities |
| 3 | Mindful Leadership Journaling | Practice self-reflection |
| 4 | Virtue Assessment and Alignment | Cultivate virtue and integrity |
| 5 | Emotional Response Analysis | Maintain emotional equilibrium |
| 6 | Success-Failure Reflection | The impermanence of outcomes |
| 7 | Team Culture Audit | Foster a strong team culture |
| 8 | Behavioral Modeling | Lead by example |
| 9 | Leadership Philosophy Canvas | All eight principles, the capstone |
