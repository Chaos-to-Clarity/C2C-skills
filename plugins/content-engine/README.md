# Content Engine

Your best ideas come up in conversations. Content Engine turns them into content you can ship every week.

Every Monday morning, Content Engine reads last week's meeting notes and:

- **Finds the pattern.** One fear, blind spot, or hard-won lesson that came up with at least two different people, chosen for what your audience struggles with most.
- **Names a framework.** Three to five parts, each with a question your reader can ask themselves. Original to you, and never repeated within 60 days.
- **Drafts your content in your voice.** A newsletter issue and a LinkedIn post by default, or add an X thread, a blog post, or a short video script.
- **Keeps every story confidential.** No names, companies, numbers, or details anyone could recognize. Stories are composited and generalized, and you get a list of which meetings it drew from so you can check.
- **Self-edits before you see it.** It strips the habits that make writing read as AI-generated, checks that every source it names is real and correctly attributed, and flags the calls it was least sure of.

The drafts land in your inbox, a Notion page, or a Google Doc, ready to edit and ship that day.

## Get started

1. Install the Content Engine plugin (see the [main page](../../README.md)). For the best results, install [Voice](../voice) too.
2. Say **"Set up Content Engine."** It connects your meeting notes, learns your voice from a few pieces of your writing (or uses your Voice skill), reads your context, picks your outputs, runs a test on last week, and schedules the weekly run.
3. Anytime: **"Run my content engine for last week."**
4. After you edit a draft, paste your version and say **"learn from this."** Each edit makes the next first draft closer.

## What it needs

- A meeting notes app connected to Claude (Granola, Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, or similar)
- Email (Gmail or Outlook), Notion, or Google Drive for delivery
- Optional: [Voice](../voice) for a voice skill that learns from your edits, and [Cortex](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex) so the engine knows your audience, positioning, and offers
- For the weekly run: a Claude plan with scheduled tasks, set to run on its own

## What it will never do

Publish, post, or send your content to anyone but you, name or quote the people you met with, invent stories, numbers, or quotes, or write about topics you've ruled out.
