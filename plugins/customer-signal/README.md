# Customer Signal

Every customer and prospect call becomes evidence, counted across accounts in your [Cortex](https://github.com/chaostoclarity/Cortex).

Founders hear the same request from five customers and remember it as "a few people mentioned it." Customer Signal reads your calls each week, pulls out what customers actually said, and folds it into themes: pains, requests, objections, churn risks, praise, and competitors. Each theme links to every account that raised it, so you can see that 9 accounts have asked for SSO, 3 of them in renewal, along with what each one said.

## How it works

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Once | Confirms your meeting notes and customer accounts, builds the Customer Signals collection, backfills the last 30 days, and schedules the weekly run |
| Weekly, on a schedule | Reads the week's customer and prospect calls, updates the themes, flags churn risks on the account, and emails you a brief: what's rising, what's new, what's blocking deals, and one move |
| Any time | Ask **"what are customers saying about onboarding?"** or **"who asked for SSO?"** |
| When you ship | Say **"we shipped SSO, close the loop."** You get a list of everyone who asked, plus a draft note to each one |

Every theme maps to one of the ten product-market fit pillars from [Growth by Design](../growth-by-design), so the brief tells you which pillar is generating the most pain.

## Get started

1. Install the Cortex and Customer Signal plugins (see the [main page](../../README.md)). Customer Signal needs a Cortex with your meeting notes connected.
2. Say: **"Set up my Cortex"** if you haven't, then **"Run customer signal."** The first run sets it up.

## What it needs

- A Cortex, with your customers and prospects in Companies (setup helps you add the main ones)
- A meeting-notes tool connected to Claude: Granola, Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, or a folder of notes
- For the weekly brief: a Claude plan with scheduled tasks

Works well with **Investor Update**, which pulls your strongest customer themes into the monthly update; with **Growth by Design**, which gives you the exercise for the pillar that's hurting; and with **Voice**, which writes the close-the-loop notes in your voice.

It never emails a customer. Briefs go to you, and notes are drafts.
