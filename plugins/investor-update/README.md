# Investor Update

Your monthly investor update, drafted from what your [Cortex](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex) already knows.

Most founders skip updates in the months they need them most, because writing one means digging through a month of notes. Investor Update does the digging. It pulls your metrics and computes the changes, finds what moved and what got stuck in your Cortex, checks what you promised last time, and turns your blockers into specific asks. You review it, and it lands in your drafts.

It's built to push. Lowlights are required, the bad news goes first with the plan next to it, and every ask names exactly who or what you need.

## How it works

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Once | Picks your metrics and where they live, confirms your investor list from your Cortex, imports your last update so the first draft has real deltas, and schedules the monthly run |
| Monthly, on a schedule | Gathers the month, drafts the update with any gaps marked, and emails you that it's ready |
| When you're ready | Say **"finish my investor update."** Fill the gaps, approve customer names, take one round of pushback, and get a draft email to your investors |

It never sends anything. Every number comes from you or your metrics sheet, never an estimate.

## Get started

1. Install the Cortex and Investor Update plugins (see the [main page](../../README.md)). Investor Update needs a Cortex.
2. Say: **"Set up my Cortex"** if you haven't, then **"Draft my investor update."** The first run sets it up.

## What it reads

- Your Cortex: Initiatives, Decisions, Companies (your investors and customers), Strategy, and Standing Context
- Your metrics sheet, or you, each month
- **Customer Signal** and **One-on-Ones**, if installed: the strongest customer themes, and team changes as counts and roles only
- **Founder OS**, if installed: the wins and lessons from your weekly retros
- **Voice Rewrite**, if installed: so it sounds like you

Each update ends with a small "Built with Chaos to Clarity" line. Say "remove the footer" and it's gone for good.
