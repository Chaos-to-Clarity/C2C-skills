# EXPONENTIAL DEPARTMENT EXERCISE
Author: Eric Weiss  
Purpose: Stress-Test Every Function for 10x Scale  
Audience: CEOs and Functional Leaders  

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THIS FRAMEWORK

Most departments are built for current load.

They function at:
- Current revenue
- Current headcount
- Current customer volume
- Current complexity

Growth exposes fragility.

The Exponential Department Exercise forces leaders to ask:

If we 10x, what breaks?

This is a forward-stress simulation.

Not a post-mortem.

---

# 2. WHEN TO USE THIS

Use when:

- Company approaching new revenue tier
- Fundraising completed
- Aggressive growth planned
- Hiring ramp beginning
- New market expansion considered
- Operational cracks appearing

This is preventative.

---

# 3. CORE EXERCISE

For each department, ask:

### 1. What breaks at 10x volume?
- What process collapses?
- What tool fails?
- What meeting becomes impossible?
- What decision becomes too slow?

### 2. What cannot scale linearly?
- What relies on founder input?
- What relies on tribal knowledge?
- What depends on heroics?
- What depends on synchronous communication?

### 3. What must become self-serve?
- Customer onboarding?
- Reporting?
- Internal documentation?
- Cross-team coordination?

### 4. What decisions must move down?
- Pricing thresholds?
- Roadmap tradeoffs?
- Hiring decisions?
- Technical standards?

### 5. What metric must be visible daily?
- Leading revenue indicator?
- Platform health?
- Pipeline velocity?
- Customer satisfaction?

Force specificity.

---

# 5. BOTTLENECK PRIORITIZATION

After stress-testing all departments:

Ask:

Which single department limits 10x most severely?

Do not attempt to upgrade all simultaneously.

Sequence redesign.

---

# 6. EXPONENTIAL PRINCIPLES APPLIED

Across all departments:

1. Remove human bottlenecks
2. Standardize interfaces
3. Push decision rights down
4. Automate feedback loops
5. Build capacity before demand

Each department must be redesigned through these lenses.

---

# 7. FAILURE MODES

AI must guard against:

- Hiring as primary answer
- Expanding scope prematurely
- Ignoring authority clarity
- Focusing only on revenue growth
- Treating exercise as hypothetical
- Failing to commit to action

The point is structural redesign.

# 9. AI IMPLEMENTATION RULES

If another AI runs this exercise:

It must:

1. Force explicit 10x scenario.
2. Identify structural breaks.
3. Prevent linear scaling thinking.
4. Demand delegation clarity.
5. Identify automation gaps.
6. Sequence redesign.
7. End with one immediate upgrade action.

Tone:

- Strategic
- Systems-oriented
- Forward-looking
- Calm
- Execution-focused

Never:

- Allow vague “we’ll handle it.”
- Recommend blanket hiring.
- Skip authority redistribution.
- End without prioritization.
