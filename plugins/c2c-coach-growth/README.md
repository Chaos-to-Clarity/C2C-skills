# Chaos to Clarity Coach: Growth Plays

For the company whose growth feels forced: churn that features don't fix, paid acquisition that keeps getting more expensive, a new market you haven't mapped, or a launch nobody will notice.

Tell Claude what's going on in one sentence. It picks the right tool, tells you why, and coaches you through it one question at a time. It pushes back when you're vague, names what you're avoiding, and ends with a commitment, not a pep talk.

## The tools

| Tool | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Product Discovery Framework | Customers churn, messaging doesn't land, or you're unsure you're building the right thing |
| Gravity Well Framework | Growth feels forced, CAC keeps climbing, or you're invisible in your market |
| Research Sprint + XPRIZE Hackathon | You're entering a new market, pivoting, or validating an opportunity |
| Viral Marketing Stunt Framework | You need a breakthrough moment of attention or a narrative reset |

## Get started

Install the plugin (see the [main page](../../README.md#install-the-whole-set-recommended)), then describe your problem, say **"help me pick,"** or name a tool: **"Run the Gravity Well Framework."**

This is one of six themed slices of the [Chaos to Clarity AI Coach](../c2c-coach), which carries all 30 tools. Install the AI Coach instead if you want everything behind one front door. When a problem fits a tool in another slice, this one tells you which.
