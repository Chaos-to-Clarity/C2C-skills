### Team Sprint Retrospective Framework
File: `tools/team-sprint-retrospective.md`

**Problems**: Retros that feel pointless. Same team issues recurring sprint after sprint. Venting without action. Quiet team members never speaking up. Action items that never get done. No team charter or stale charter. Engineering or product team stuck in unproductive patterns.
**Deploy when**: A team's retros are broken, a new team needs a real cadence, or chronic patterns need to be codified into team agreements. For solo weekly reflection, use the weekly-retro skill in Founder OS.
**Tags**: Team, Execution, Process, Engineering, Product, Facilitation
