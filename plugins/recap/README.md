# Meeting Recap

Every meeting becomes done work.

After each meeting, Meeting Recap reads the notes and:

- **Sends a recap email** in your brand and voice to the people you met, with you copied. Decisions, open questions, and next steps with owners and dates. Sensitive meetings (hiring, legal, negotiations) always wait as drafts.
- **Captures your to-dos** in your task list and books time to do them before they're due, within your working hours, with travel buffers around in-person meetings.
- **Drafts the follow-ups you promised:** the document you said you'd send, the intro you offered.
- **Books the meetings you agreed to.** "Let's grab coffee next week" becomes a calendar invite at one of your spots.
- **Does small tasks itself:** the research, agenda, or outline you mentioned, ready and linked.
- **Remembers what matters.** New people, shifted projects, and decisions go to your [Cortex](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex), if you have one.

It runs twice a day on its own, and emails you only when a draft needs your review.

## Get started

1. Install the Meeting Recap plugin (see the [main page](../../README.md)).
2. Say **"Set up Meeting Recap."** It connects your meeting notes, email, and calendar, sets your branding and how much it does on its own, sends you a test on your last meeting, and schedules the routine.
3. Anytime: **"Recap my last meeting"** or **"Recap my call with Sam."**

## What it needs

- A meeting notes app connected to Claude (Granola, Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, or similar)
- Email (Gmail or Outlook) and a calendar connected to Claude
- Optional: [Cortex](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex) for memory and [Founder OS](../founder-os) for tasks
- For the twice-daily routine: a Claude plan with scheduled tasks, set to run on its own

## What it will never do

Spend money or book paid reservations, accept terms or sign anything, commit you to something you didn't agree to, email people who weren't in the meeting (except intros you promised, as drafts), or delete anything.
