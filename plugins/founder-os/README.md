# Founder OS

The Founder Operating System: a weekly cycle that keeps you working on what matters.

Plan on Monday. Get briefed every morning. Reflect on Friday. Each step reads your [Cortex](https://github.com/Chaos-to-Clarity/Cortex) for your priorities, people, and strategy, takes you through the work, and writes your answers back, so the system and the Cortex stay current.

It's built to push. It ties goals to strategy, checks them against the hours you actually have, and names anything you've carried for weeks without finishing.

## The cycle

| When | Skill | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Once | `founder-os-setup` | Builds Weekly Goals, Tasks, Reflections, and Commitments next to your Cortex, and schedules the routines |
| Monday | `weekly-planning` | Carries forward last week, sets three to five goals, pressure-tests them, writes the plan, and blocks the time |
| Each work day | `daily-briefing` | Your schedule with context, meeting prep, open tasks, inbox flags, a straight priorities check, and one move. Ends with a quick task check-in |
| Friday | `weekly-retro` | Scores the week, asks ten reflection questions, names recurring patterns, and sets next week's commitment |

The daily briefing runs fully on a schedule. Planning and the retro are conversations, so their scheduled runs prepare everything and ping you to finish when you're ready.

## Get started

1. Install the Cortex and Founder OS plugins (see the [main page](../../README.md)). Founder OS needs Cortex.
2. Say: **"Set up my Cortex"**, then **"Set up Founder OS."**
3. On Monday, say: **"Plan my week."**

## What it needs

- A Cortex (setup builds a minimal one if you don't have one yet)
- Your calendar connected to Claude; meeting notes and email make the briefing much better
- For the morning briefing and the Monday and Friday prep: a Claude plan with scheduled tasks
