# Diagnose mode

For founders who know something is off but not which pillar. Goal: get from their symptoms to the constraint pillar and the right exercise in five to eight questions, then confirm with that pillar's quick check.

Diagnose is a fast way in, not a substitute for the PMF Scorecard. If the founder has no full scorecard yet, or the last one is 90 or more days old, recommend it once after the verdict, so the plan rests on all ten pillars. If they have a recent one, use it instead.

## Flow

**Skip what you already know.** If the founder's message already describes the symptom, don't ask for it again. If the PMF Profile has their stage, don't ask for it. If they have scores, use them: a symptom that lands on a pillar they scored low confirms it; one that lands on a pillar they scored high means either the score was generous or the real constraint sits upstream.

**Question 1: Symptom.** Open-ended, unless their message already gave it.
> What's happening that tells you something's off? Describe it the way you'd tell a co-founder at 11pm.

If the answer is vague ("growth is slow", "it's not working"), ask for one recent, concrete example: "Tell me about the last deal you lost, or the last customer who churned. What happened?" If it's still vague, offer the symptom menu below as options.

**Question 2: Stage**, only if it's not in the profile. Use AskUserQuestion when available, otherwise number the options. Use the five stages from `references/storage.md`.

**Questions 3 to 6: Tell the pillars apart.** Match their words to the symptom table. Most symptoms point at two candidate pillars. Ask the discriminating question from the table, one at a time, until one pillar clearly leads. Ask for numbers when they exist (interviews in the last 90 days, conversion rates, CAC, churn), and accept "I don't know" as a finding: missing evidence is itself a signal.

**Then give the verdict:**

> **Your constraint looks like [Pillar].** Here's why: [one or two sentences connecting their exact words to the pillar].
>
> What would prove me wrong: [the evidence that would point elsewhere].
>
> Start with **#N [Exercise]** (Chapter N, "[Title]"). Then **#M [Exercise]**.
>
> Want to confirm with a three-minute quick check of [Pillar] first, or go straight into #N?

Then the coaching line (see SKILL.md, calls to action). If they confirm, run the quick pillar check from `references/exercises/01-pmf-scorecard.md`, save it to the profile, and start the exercise.

## Stage defaults

When symptoms are thin, the stage sets the starting point, because pillars build upstream.

| Stage | Usually the constraint | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idea, no product yet | Persona and Problem (no evidence yet) | #3 DSBA Interview Guide, #7 Persona Canvas, #11 Problem Canvas |
| Building or just launched an MVP | Solution and Experience | #13 Validation Canvas, #6 Yellow Brick Road, #14 Experience Canvas |
| Early customers, still finding fit | Outcomes, Experience, and whether the Persona is really one segment | #26 PMF Milestones Checklist, #15 Outcome Canvas, #7 Persona Canvas |
| Paying customers, but growth is hard | Depends on symptom; check Model and Channel early | The symptom table |
| Scaling | Readiness gaps | #35 Go-to-Market Readiness, #26 PMF Milestones, #1 PMF Scorecard |

## Symptom menu (when they can't put it into words)

Offer these as options, grouped:
- **Getting customers:** prospects ghost us; nobody commits; we can't say who it's for; we don't know where customers come from; deals stall on price
- **Keeping customers:** they don't activate; they churn; they don't reach a result
- **Standing out:** they can't tell us apart; the website doesn't land; we're getting copied
- **How we build:** we ship things nobody uses; the roadmap is driven by opinions; we don't know if anything worked; the MVP keeps growing
- **Big picture:** we don't know if we have PMF; we're about to scale; we've tried everything

## Rules

- Never name more than two pillars. If symptoms point at three or more, say so and recommend the full PMF Scorecard (#1). That's the honest answer.
- Always name what would prove the diagnosis wrong. It keeps the founder thinking, not just accepting.
- Remember Chapter 11's warning: a weak business model can look exactly like weak product-market fit (churn, stalled growth). When the symptom is churn or stalled growth, ask one Model question before concluding.
- Upstream first. If a downstream symptom (Messaging, Channel) sits on top of an unvalidated Persona or Problem, say the upstream pillar is the real constraint.
- Save the diagnosis to the PMF Profile under Priority pillars, marked "from diagnose," with the date. No profile yet: hold it in the conversation (see "Before storage exists" in `references/storage.md`).
