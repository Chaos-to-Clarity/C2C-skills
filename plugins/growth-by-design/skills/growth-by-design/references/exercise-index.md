The book groups the pillars two ways. Chapter 2 and the scorecard split them into five you build (Product) and five you sell (Market). The chapters run in the order you'd do the work: Persona first, then Problem, Solution, Experience, Outcomes, Differentiation, Messaging, Channel, Model, Timing. That chapter order is also the upstream order for breaking ties.
