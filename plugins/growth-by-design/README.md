# Growth by Design

35 product-market fit exercises from the book ***Growth by Design: A Product-Market Fit Playbook*** by Eric Weiss.

Product-market fit isn't luck, and it isn't a feeling. It's ten pillars that have to work together: five you build (Problem, Solution, Experience, Differentiation, Outcomes) and five you sell (Persona, Messaging, Channel, Model, Timing). This plugin is the book's workbook, run as a coach. Score all ten pillars, find the one capping your growth, and work through the canvas that fixes it, one question at a time, using your real customer evidence.

**Get the full ebook and workbook free at [growthbydesignbook.com](https://growthbydesignbook.com).** Every exercise maps to a chapter, so read along as you go.

## What it does

- **Onboarding.** A few questions about your company, then you choose where your work is saved: your Cortex, Notion, Google Drive, a folder on your computer, or just the chat.
- **The PMF Scorecard.** Fifty statements across the ten pillars, scored on evidence, not gut. You get your PMF Radar, what each score means, a priority plan for your two weakest pillars, and a tracker to re-score every quarter. Start here.
- **Diagnose.** Describe what's going wrong ("prospects love the demo, then ghost us") and get pointed at the pillar and the exercise.
- **35 exercises,** indexed by chapter. Each one saves a finished canvas, coaching notes, the assumptions you still need to test, and your next move.

## Get started

Install the plugin (see the [main page](../../README.md)), then say **"Start Growth by Design."** You'll be set up, then pointed to the PMF Scorecard. Already know where it hurts? Say **"diagnose."** Want the list? Say **"show exercises."**

Pairs well with [Cortex](https://github.com/chaostoclarity/Cortex), so your PMF Profile and canvases sit where every AI can see them, and the [Chaos to Clarity AI Coach](../c2c-coach) for leadership and team problems.
