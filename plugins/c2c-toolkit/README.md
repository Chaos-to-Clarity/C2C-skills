# Chaos to Clarity Toolkit

Thirty leadership exercises for founders, from ten years of coaching venture-backed CEOs and CTOs.

Tell Claude what's going on in one sentence ("I've needed to fire my VP of Eng for three months and I keep finding reasons to wait"). It picks the right tool, tells you why, and coaches you through it one question at a time. It pushes back when you're vague, names what you're avoiding, and ends with a commitment, not a pep talk.

## Get started

Install the plugin (see the [main page](../../README.md)), then say: **"Run the Chaos to Clarity Toolkit."** Or name a tool: **"Run the Toleration Audit."**
