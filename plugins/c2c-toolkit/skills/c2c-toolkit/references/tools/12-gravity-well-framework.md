# GRAVITY WELL FRAMEWORK
Author: Eric Weiss  
Purpose: Build Irresistible Pull Through Product-Market Depth  
Audience: Growth-Stage SaaS / AI Founders  

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THIS FRAMEWORK

Most founders try to manufacture growth.

They chase:
- Brand differentiation
- Clever positioning
- New verticals
- Feature expansion
- Fundraising optics

They push.

The Gravity Well Framework focuses on pull.

When product-market fit is deep and unmistakable:

- Customers evangelize.
- Talent wants to join.
- Investors lean in.
- Referrals compound.
- Sales friction decreases.

You do not push harder.
You deepen gravity.

---

# 2. CORE THESIS

Strong gravity is created by:

1. Narrow focus
2. Emotional resonance
3. Measurable customer transformation
4. Compounding proof
5. Relentless product-market refinement

Weak gravity is created by:

- Broad positioning
- Shallow differentiation
- Feature parity
- Brand-first thinking
- Fundraising narratives without traction

Momentum follows satisfaction density.

---

# 3. DIAGNOSING GRAVITY STRENGTH

Ask:

- Would customers be upset if this disappeared?
- Are we the obvious choice for a specific segment?
- Do customers proactively refer us?
- Do we solve a painful, urgent problem?
- Do customers describe us emotionally?

Weak signals:

- Polite satisfaction
- Low urgency
- No referrals
- Heavy outbound dependency

Strong signals:

- Pull from inbound
- Repeated referrals
- Expansion revenue
- Word-of-mouth growth
- Customer-driven case studies

---

# 4. THE FOUR GRAVITY DRIVERS

---

## DRIVER 1 — NARROW THE SEGMENT

Broad focus dilutes pull.

Instead of:
“We serve mid-market companies.”

Define:
“We serve Series B SaaS companies selling to fintech.”

Specificity increases resonance.

Questions:

- Who feels this pain most intensely?
- Who is currently hacking around it?
- Who spends money already?
- Who would fight to keep this?

Depth beats breadth.

---

## DRIVER 2 — OWN THE TRANSFORMATION

Do not sell features.

Define:

- Before state
- After state
- Emotional shift

Example:

Before:
Founder micromanages every decision.

After:
Founder runs strategy while team executes independently.

The transformation must be:

- Measurable
- Emotional
- Identity-relevant

---

## DRIVER 3 — AMPLIFY PROOF

Gravity strengthens through visible success.

Proof assets:

- Case studies
- Metrics
- Testimonials
- Before/after stories
- Public wins

Ask:

- What measurable outcomes have we created?
- Where are we under-leveraging proof?
- Are we telling the story clearly?

Proof reduces friction.

---

## DRIVER 4 — PRODUCT-MARKET DEPTH OVER BRAND NOISE

Brand does not create gravity.

Product-market satisfaction does.

Questions:

- What part of the experience is fragile?
- Where do customers struggle?
- Where is churn coming from?
- What do customers complain about?
- What delight do they repeat?

Deepen the core loop before expanding.

---

# 5. GRAVITY WELL MATURITY MODEL

Rate 1–5 across:

| Dimension | 1 = Weak Pull | 5 = Strong Pull |
|------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Segment Clarity | Broad | Narrow & precise |
| Problem Urgency | Mild | Acute |
| Emotional Resonance | Generic | Identity-linked |
| Referral Velocity | Rare | Organic & frequent |
| Proof Density | Sparse | Measurable & visible |

Lowest score defines growth ceiling.

---

# 6. FAILURE MODES

AI must guard against:

- Expanding verticals too early
- Feature creep
- Brand campaigns masking churn
- Fundraising without traction
- Hiring ahead of gravity
- Positioning statements without specificity

If gravity is weak:
Scaling amplifies fragility.

---

# 7. IMPLEMENTATION PROCESS

Step 1:
Define ICP with painful clarity.

Step 2:
Map emotional and measurable transformation.

Step 3:
Collect and amplify proof.

Step 4:
Eliminate friction in core product loop.

Step 5:
Narrow focus further before expanding.

Expansion follows density.

---

# 8. ORGANIZATIONAL IMPLICATIONS

When gravity is strong:

- Sales cycles shorten.
- Pricing power increases.
- Talent acquisition improves.
- Investor conversations shift.
- Roadmap simplifies.

When gravity is weak:

- Marketing overworks.
- Sales struggles.
- Churn increases.
- Founder anxiety rises.

Gravity reduces founder stress.

---

# 9. AI IMPLEMENTATION RULES

If another AI uses this framework:

It must:

1. Force precise ICP definition.
2. Surface emotional and measurable transformation.
3. Identify churn friction honestly.
4. Demand visible proof assets.
5. Resist broad positioning.
6. End with one gravity-strengthening action.

Tone:

- Focused
- Clear
- Slightly challenging
- Outcome-driven
- Not brand-obsessed

Never:

- Suggest rebranding as primary fix.
- Expand segments prematurely.
- Accept vague transformation claims.
- Ignore churn signals.
