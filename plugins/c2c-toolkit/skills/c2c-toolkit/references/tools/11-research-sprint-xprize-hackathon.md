# RESEARCH SPRINT + XPRIZE HACKATHON MODEL
Author: Eric Weiss  
Purpose: Structured Opportunity Discovery and Breakthrough Activation  
Audience: Founders, Innovation Teams, Venture Studios, Ecosystem Builders  

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THIS FRAMEWORK

Most innovation efforts fail because they start with solutions.

Teams brainstorm.
They ideate features.
They build prototypes.

They skip disciplined problem excavation.

The Research Sprint + XPRIZE Hackathon Model creates:

- Domain clarity
- High-leverage problem selection
- Public accountability
- Ecosystem engagement
- Signal amplification

It turns vague ambition into structured breakthrough challenges.

---

# 2. WHEN TO USE THIS

Use when:

- Entering a new domain
- Pivoting direction
- Exploring adjacent markets
- Validating opportunity space
- Launching ecosystem initiative
- Designing viral attention event

Do not use for:

- Minor feature iteration
- Routine roadmap planning
- Internal-only brainstorming

This is for directional clarity.

---

# 3. PHASE 1 — DOMAIN CLARIFICATION

Define:

- What domain are we exploring?
- Why does this matter strategically?
- What macro trends are relevant?
- What capabilities do we uniquely possess?
- What adjacent expertise do we need?

Output:

Clear domain framing statement.

Example:

"We are exploring identity verification in decentralized systems with an emphasis on fraud resistance and privacy."

Without domain clarity:
Research diffuses.

---

# 4. PHASE 2 — RAPID RESEARCH SPRINT (1–2 WEEKS)

Objective:

Map the problem landscape before proposing solutions.

Activities:

1. Expert Interviews
   - Practitioners
   - Operators
   - Regulators
   - Customers

2. Competitive Landscape Review
   - Existing players
   - Failed startups
   - Substitute solutions
   - Workarounds

3. Pain Mapping
   - Workflow breakdowns
   - Emotional friction
   - Economic loss
   - Operational inefficiencies

4. Constraint Identification
   - Technical limitations
   - Regulatory barriers
   - Behavioral inertia
   - Capital constraints

Ask repeatedly:

- What actually sucks here?
- What is costly but tolerated?
- What is politically sensitive?
- What is emotionally charged?
- What is currently hacked together?

Output:

Structured Problem Map.

---

# 5. PHASE 3 — LEVERAGE POINT IDENTIFICATION

From the problem map, extract:

- High-intensity pain
- Large economic impact
- Low solution quality
- High emotional frustration
- Strategic alignment with capabilities

Filter using:

| Criteria | Score 1–5 |
|----------|------------|
| Pain intensity |
| Market size |
| Urgency |
| Competitive saturation |
| Strategic fit |

Select top leverage problem.

Avoid trying to solve multiple.

---

# 6. PHASE 4 — XPRIZE-STYLE CHALLENGE DESIGN

Convert leverage problem into public challenge.

Structure:

## Challenge Statement

Clear, measurable, ambitious.

Example:

"Design a decentralized identity verification system that reduces fraud by 90% while preserving user privacy and operating under 500ms response time."

## Success Criteria

- Measurable outcomes
- Performance thresholds
- Time constraints
- Resource constraints

## Prize Structure

- Monetary reward
- Pilot opportunity
- Public recognition
- Partnership access

## Timeline

- Announcement date
- Submission deadline
- Judging period
- Winner announcement

The challenge must:

- Be hard but achievable
- Force creativity
- Attract talent
- Generate attention

---

# 7. PHASE 5 — PUBLIC ACTIVATION

Leverage challenge for:

- Brand authority
- Ecosystem engagement
- Media coverage
- Community building
- Talent discovery

Components:

- Landing page
- Video announcement
- Partner endorsements
- Social amplification
- Demo day event

The event becomes signal amplification engine.

---

# 8. PHASE 6 — POST-CHALLENGE INTEGRATION

After hackathon:

- Evaluate submissions
- Identify breakthrough ideas
- Offer pilots or partnerships
- Extract insights into roadmap
- Publish results publicly

Do not let event die without integration.

---

# 9. VIRAL STUNT OVERLAY (OPTIONAL)

To amplify attention:

Tie challenge to:

- Emotional story
- Cultural tension
- Public spectacle
- Performance art
- Symbolic demonstration

Requirements:

- Direct link to problem
- High emotional resonance
- Media-friendly framing
- Clear narrative arc

The stunt must serve strategic objective.
Not vanity.

---

# 10. FAILURE MODES

AI must guard against:

- Solution-first thinking
- Weak challenge statements
- Overly broad scope
- No measurable success criteria
- No integration plan
- Pure PR event without substance

Innovation without discipline creates noise.

---

# 11. AI IMPLEMENTATION RULES

If another AI runs this framework:

It must:

1. Force domain clarity before research.
2. Structure research outputs.
3. Score leverage opportunities.
4. Demand measurable challenge criteria.
5. Ensure alignment with strategic goals.
6. Design activation plan.
7. Define integration pathway.
8. End with execution timeline.

Tone:

- Strategic
- Structured
- Ambitious
- Analytical
- Execution-focused

Never:

- Jump to solution prematurely.
- Allow vague problem statements.
- Design challenge without measurable criteria.
- Separate PR from strategy.
