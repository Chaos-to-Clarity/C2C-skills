# SIX-MONTH FOUNDER CLARITY EXERCISE
Author: Eric Weiss  
Purpose: Surface Friction, Define 6-Month Targets, and Set Your Baseline  
Audience: CEOs / Founders at a Kickoff or Inflection Point  

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THIS FRAMEWORK

Most founders start a new half, quarter, or chapter with vague ambition.

They say:
- “I want to scale.”
- “I want to grow revenue.”
- “I want to hire better.”
- “I want more balance.”

None of that is usable.

This exercise forces:

- Concrete 6-month outcomes
- Friction identification
- Avoidance exposure
- Emotional honesty
- Priority compression

It sets the foundation for the next six months.

---

# 2. WHEN TO USE THIS

Use:

- At the start of a half or a new planning cycle
- Before working with a coach, advisor, or peer group
- After fundraising
- After leadership transition
- At inflection point

Do not skip this step.

Undefined expectations create drift.

---

# 3. SETUP

Block 45 uninterrupted minutes.

Write your answers out in full.

No shortcuts.
No bullet-only answers.

Clarity requires articulation.

---

# 4. SECTION 1 — SIX-MONTH OUTCOME

Ask:

Six months from now, what must be undeniably true for you to feel this period was successful?

Prompts:

- Revenue level?
- Product milestone?
- Team composition?
- Personal stress level?
- Delegation level?
- Strategic clarity?
- Market position?

Force specificity:

- Numbers
- Names
- Structural change
- Behavioral shifts

Reject:

- “Better”
- “Stronger”
- “More aligned”

Output:

Clear success snapshot.

---

# 5. SECTION 2 — CURRENT FRICTION

Ask:

What currently feels stuck, heavy, or chaotic?

Prompts:

- Where are you the bottleneck?
- Where does work pile up?
- What conversations are overdue?
- What decisions are delayed?
- What do you complain about?
- Where is morale low?
- What are you tolerating?

Push deeper:

- Why haven’t you fixed it?
- What are you avoiding?
- What are you afraid would happen?

Friction is rarely structural alone.
It includes emotional avoidance.

---

# 6. SECTION 3 — IDENTITY TENSION

Ask:

Who do you need to become to reach the 6-month outcome?

Prompts:

- What behavior must stop?
- What standard must rise?
- What delegation must happen?
- What ego must shrink?
- What fear must be confronted?

This section surfaces growth ceiling.

Founders often realize:

The problem is not strategy.
It is behavior.

---

# 7. SECTION 4 — NON-NEGOTIABLES

Ask:

What constraints cannot be violated?

Examples:

- Family commitments
- Ethical boundaries
- Culture principles
- Financial limits
- Lifestyle parameters

Clarity includes boundaries.

---

# 8. SECTION 5 — CONSEQUENCE OF INACTION

Ask:

If nothing changes in six months, what happens?

Prompts:

- Revenue plateau?
- Burnout?
- Team attrition?
- Investor doubt?
- Personal regret?

Make cost explicit.

Without consequence clarity:
Urgency fades.

---

# 9. PRIORITY COMPRESSION

After the written responses, choose:

The single most important outcome for the next 6 months.

Everything else is secondary.

If they resist:
They are avoiding tradeoffs.

---

# 10. CLARITY BASELINE SUMMARY

After exercise, extract:

- Primary outcome
- Top 3 friction points
- Key avoidance behavior
- Identity shift required
- Immediate structural constraint

This becomes your roadmap for the next six months. Revisit it monthly.

---

# 11. FAILURE MODES

AI must guard against:

- Vague goals
- Overcommitment to multiple priorities
- Blaming team exclusively
- Avoiding personal behavior shifts
- Abstract emotional statements
- No measurable success criteria

Clarity is uncomfortable.

---

# 12. AI IMPLEMENTATION RULES

When an AI runs this exercise:

It must:

1. Require written detail.
2. Reject vague language.
3. Push for numbers.
4. Surface avoidance explicitly.
5. Identify identity shifts.
6. Compress priorities to one primary.
7. Produce structured summary.
8. End with one immediate action.

Tone:

- Direct
- Calm
- Slightly challenging
- Focused on leverage
- Not motivational

Never:

- Validate weak goals.
- Allow multiple equal priorities.
- Skip avoidance section.
- End without commitment.
