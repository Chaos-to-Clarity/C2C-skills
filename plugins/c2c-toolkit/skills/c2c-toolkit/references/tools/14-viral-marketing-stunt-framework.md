# VIRAL MARKETING STUNT FRAMEWORK
Author: Eric Weiss  
Purpose: High-Impact Narrative Activation Tied to Strategic Leverage  
Audience: Founders, Venture-Backed Startups, Ecosystem Builders  

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THIS FRAMEWORK

Most startups confuse marketing with visibility.

They run ads.
They publish content.
They attend conferences.

They remain forgettable.

A Viral Marketing Stunt is:

- Strategically engineered
- Emotionally charged
- Narratively sharp
- Directly tied to customer pain
- Amplified through spectacle

This is not random attention seeking.
This is controlled detonation.

---

# 2. CORE THESIS

Attention without narrative is noise.

Narrative without tension is forgettable.

Tension without product alignment is vanity.

A proper stunt must:

- Highlight a painful truth
- Make the invisible visible
- Embody the problem physically or socially
- Point clearly to your solution

The stunt serves strategy.

---

# 3. WHEN TO USE THIS

Use when:

- Entering crowded market
- Launching new category
- Pivoting narrative
- Activating ecosystem
- Reinforcing brand authority
- Announcing breakthrough

Do not use when:

- Product fragile
- Messaging unclear
- No clear ICP
- Team cannot handle attention spike

Amplifying weakness accelerates failure.

---

# 4. STUNT DESIGN CRITERIA

Every stunt must meet five requirements:

1. Directly tied to customer pain
2. Visually or socially dramatic
3. Easily understood in one sentence
4. Media-friendly
5. Leads directly into product narrative

If any element missing:
Redesign.

---

# 5. STEP 1 — IDENTIFY THE CORE TENSION

Ask:

- What frustrates our ICP deeply?
- What hypocrisy exists in this industry?
- What inefficiency is tolerated?
- What systemic failure is ignored?
- What cultural contradiction exists?

The tension must feel:

- Uncomfortable
- Real
- Urgent

Example tensions:

- Bureaucracy vs speed
- Surveillance vs privacy
- Fragmentation vs simplicity
- Burnout vs automation

Without tension:
No viral spread.

---

# 6. STEP 2 — PHYSICAL OR SOCIAL EMBODIMENT

Translate tension into action.

Options:

- Public demonstration
- Data reveal
- Social experiment
- Performance art
- Symbolic destruction
- Public challenge
- Record attempt
- Pop-up experience

The embodiment must:

- Make abstract problem concrete
- Create conversation
- Invite participation
- Trigger emotion

Example:

If identity fraud rampant,
Host live public challenge demonstrating detection failure.

---

# 7. STEP 3 — NARRATIVE ARC

Structure:

1. Problem revelation
2. Emotional amplification
3. Spectacle moment
4. Resolution pathway
5. Product positioning

Without narrative arc:
Event dissipates.

---

# 8. STEP 4 — AMPLIFICATION STRATEGY

Channels:

- Influencer partnerships
- Industry press
- Social clips
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Livestream
- Partner endorsements
- Community engagement

Build anticipation.

Do not launch quietly.

---

# 9. STEP 5 — PRODUCT INTEGRATION

The stunt must transition into:

- Demo
- Waitlist
- Pilot program
- Beta invite
- Partnership announcement
- Challenge participation

Attention must convert.

If stunt overshadows product:
It backfires.

---

# 10. RISK MANAGEMENT

Ask:

- What could go wrong?
- What backlash could occur?
- What legal exposure exists?
- What operational capacity required?
- What reputational risk exists?

Design mitigation plan.

Controversy can amplify.
Uncontrolled chaos destroys.

---

# 11. FAILURE MODES

AI must guard against:

- Stunts detached from strategy
- Attention without follow-up
- No measurable outcome
- Over-complex concept
- Ego-driven spectacle
- PR-only thinking
- No product tie-in

If stunt does not advance leverage:
It is distraction.

---

# 12. VIRAL INTENSITY CHECK

Score 1–5:

| Dimension | Score |
|------------|-------|
| Emotional tension |
| Clarity of narrative |
| Visual impact |
| Product alignment |
| Media friendliness |

Minimum threshold average: 4.

---

# 13. AI IMPLEMENTATION RULES

If another AI uses this framework:

It must:

1. Identify clear customer pain.
2. Extract underlying tension.
3. Propose embodiment concept.
4. Map narrative arc explicitly.
5. Design amplification plan.
6. Define product integration step.
7. Identify risk mitigation.
8. Provide execution timeline.

Tone:

- Bold
- Strategic
- Controlled
- High-leverage oriented
- Not reckless

Never:

- Suggest random spectacle.
- Separate stunt from product.
- Ignore risk.
- Create ego-driven theatrics.
